# Autonomy conditions (§4.2)

| Condition | Human role | Sessions |
|---|---|---|
| Autonomous | Sets bounds, no further contact | 290 (Table 1) |
| Checkpoint | Approves or redirects only at two gates: after the plan, after the first result | 12 |
| Human-assisted | Works the paper with the agent, may step in at any point | 12 |

Each of six students completes two checkpoint and two human-assisted sessions on a shared subset of four papers. Assignments are randomized and no student sees the same paper twice. Every intervention and its cause is logged.

Assignments live in `experiments/human-conditions/assignments.csv`.
